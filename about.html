About the Band

Biography

Toys That Bïte is a powerhouse band that fuses the raw energy of hard rock with the intensity of heavy metal. Forged from the brainchild of two long time rockers Guitar/Vocals - Karl Bauers (former Metal Gods) and Lead Vocals/Drums - Adam Stanley (former Whailtale, King Walrus & Communicators) passionate about the glory days of solid guitar riffs and metal ass-kickery.

Joined by Jason Shepard (Bass Guitar), Terry Anderson (Drums) and Dave Rivett (Guitar/Vocals) this band of calamitous reprobates will have you banging your head and feeling like you’ve been hit by a juggernaut.

Toys That Bïte lays tribute to the supercharged sound of 80’s and 90’s rock drawing inspiration from legendary acts like Judas Priest, Iron Maiden, Dio, Aerosmith, Alice Cooper and many more. Their fans (affectionately known as ‘Biters’) have characterised their music as thunderous guitar riffs, pounding drums, and vocal lines that have you hooked on repeat for days to come.

Whether you’re a die-hard metalhead or a casual rock fan, Toys That Bïte promises to leave you breathless and craving more. Brace yourself for a musical journey that defies expectations and leaves you with an unforgettable experience.

You have been warned!!
